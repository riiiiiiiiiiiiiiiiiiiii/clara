How Phases Are Calculated
Understanding your four seasons
Menstrual Days 1–5 (approx)
Hormone levels are at their lowest. The uterine lining sheds. A time for rest and inward reflection.
Follicular Days 6–13 (approx)
Oestrogen rises as follicles develop. Energy builds, mood lifts, creativity flourishes.
Ovulatory Days 14–16 (approx)
LH surges triggering egg release. Oestrogen and testosterone peak. Confidence and sociability are highest.
Luteal Days 17–28 (approx)
Progesterone rises then falls. Energy slows, sensitivity increases. The body prepares for the next cycle.
